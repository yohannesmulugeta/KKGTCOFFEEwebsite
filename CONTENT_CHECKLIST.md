# Content and publication checklist

The public site intentionally avoids invented lot specifications and facility claims.

## Confirm before treating this as a verified company site

- Exact legal company name, office address, primary email and telephone number. The displayed channels currently follow the corporate repository's `src/data/company.ts`, whose own `DATA_REQUIRED.md` marks them for confirmation.
- For each of Guji, Nekemte / Lekempti, Yirgacheffe, Sidama, Limmu, and Jimma / Djimmah: current grade, process, crop year, quantity, packing, and availability if those details should be public. Guji was added at the user's request; confirm current lot details before making any specific offer.
- Any certifications, test results, scores, farm or facility claims, and supporting documents before displaying them.
- KKGT-owned photos or approved licensed imagery to replace the illustrative generated images where documentary proof is desired.
- Confirm each draft Team name and current title, then supply approved portraits and any short biographies to publish. The demo currently uses six draft entries from the corporate content list; the ambiguous Commercial Head spelling was left out.
- Supply approved gallery photos with captions and usage rights. Confirm any exact origin locations before replacing the illustrative map markers with factual coordinates.
- Provide verified social profile URLs before adding social links to the footer.
- A KKGT-owned video and poster if a moving hero is desired. The current site deliberately uses a still image.
- A production inquiry endpoint, consent wording, spam protection, and an end-to-end delivery test if the site should send forms rather than prepare email drafts.
- If hosting moves away from GitHub Pages, confirm the route strategy and asset base for the new domain.
